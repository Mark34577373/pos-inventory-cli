## Accounting definitions

All values use integer cents. Taxes retain each store's existing half-up rounding.

- Recorded gross sales retain existing analytics semantics: completed simulated
  sale totals, **after discounts and including tax**, on the sale business date.
- Original merchandise is the sum of original unit retail prices × quantities.
- Discounts reduce merchandise before tax. Net merchandise = original merchandise
  − allocated discounts. Existing analytics item revenue now reflects that amount.
- Refunds use original paid units and original allocated tax on the refund business
  date. Net sales = recorded gross sales − refunds, including tax.
- Sales tax is collected tax less refunded tax. Legacy reconciliation differences
  are not automatically treated as verified tax.
- Revenue excluding tax = net sales − net verified sales tax. Unverified historical
  tax must be disclosed before representing this as an exact amount.
- Product reference acquisition cost is manually maintained and snapshotted only
  for future sale lines. Missing means unknown; zero is a recorded zero cost.
  This is **not FIFO, weighted-average costing, or a perpetual valuation system**.
- Estimated COGS uses original sale cost snapshots × units sold; physical restocked
  refunds reverse their original cost. Non-restocked refunds do not restore stock
  or reverse COGS. Missing historical costs are never backfilled from current costs.
- Estimated gross profit = revenue excluding tax − estimated COGS, available only
  when the period's relevant cost and tax data are complete.
- Operating expenses are separately recorded business costs and reversals.
  Purchasing/receiving do not automatically create expenses or supplier payments.
- Estimated operating result = estimated gross profit − net operating expenses.
  Estimates are operational aids, not financial statements or cash-flow reports.

## Customers and checkout

Customers belong to exactly one store. Display name is required; email, phone and
notes are optional. Duplicate contact details are allowed, never merged. Deactivate
rather than delete. Profiles retain linked purchases, refunds, exact totals and last
purchase date; display shows the latest 100 movements with bounded complete totals.
Customer summary CSV excludes contact information and follows search/status/sort.

Checkout supports guest or active customer selection, a bounded customer search,
and existing product catalog/category/SKU search, quantity edits and cart cancellation.
Customer selection and discount are scoped to the authenticated cart context and
cleared on completion, cancellation or account change. Customer names are snapshotted
on completed transactions. Private transaction details link profiles. Printed sale
receipts omit customer contact information and acquisition costs.

Order discounts support fixed dollar amounts (at most two decimals) or percentages
(up to four decimals), rounded half up once on the order. Each unit retains at least
one cent: free-item/zero-value sales are outside the existing positive refund ledger.
Allocation weights are each unit's retail cents minus one; largest remainders resolve
by original line order then unit ordinal. Per-line base discount, bonus-unit count,
and total discount are persisted. Original tax is allocated across the resulting
paid unit prices using largest remainders and the same stable tie rule. Partial
refunds consume successive original unit ordinals, so complete refunds reconcile
exactly without expanding million-unit arrays. Cart changes recalculate server totals;
invalidated discounts require correction or removal before checkout. Browser totals
are never authoritative.

Existing product-initial placeholders are reused consistently. Image uploads,
item-level promotions and hardware integrations are deferred.

## Purchasing

Suppliers have optional contact information and are deactivated rather than deleted.
Preferred supplier and supplier SKU are optional product metadata. Reference cost is
edited separately from retail price; PO receiving never changes either automatically.

Purchase lifecycle: draft → ordered → partially received → received. Drafts may be
edited; draft or ordered orders with no receipts may be cancelled. Partially received
orders cannot be cancelled. Ordered quantities and unit cost/name snapshots lock.
Drafts and orders do not change stock. Receiving requires active products, positive
whole quantities no greater than remaining order quantities, and the existing stock
limit. Existing ordered suppliers may subsequently be deactivated without blocking
valid outstanding receiving. New orders require an active supplier.

Receiving uses `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, a store-specific durable key and request fingerprint.
Repeated identical requests return the original receipt; changed requests using that
key fail. Receiving rows, conditional product increases, immutable stock adjustments,
order state and database-triggered alert recalculation commit together or roll back.
Stock history retains adjustment type `restock` for compatibility, with a receipt
foreign key and readable PO/receiving IDs in the reason. Purchase receipts are immutable.

Ordered metrics exclude drafts/cancellations and use PO creation business dates;
received metrics use receiving business dates, independently of order creation date.
Partial receipts are counted once. Neither metric asserts that a supplier was paid.
Supplier and PO exports have explicit bounds and neutralize spreadsheet formulas.
Purchase returns are deferred: there is no incomplete return or supplier-credit action.

## Migration and safety

Schema 24 adds customers, optional transaction association/name snapshots and discount
snapshots. Schema 25 adds suppliers, optional product reference costs, future sale cost
snapshots, purchase orders/items, receiving records/items and stock receiving links.
Schema 26 adds the immutable expense/reversal ledger. This schema number is not the
planned Day 26 visual redesign.
Migrations are additive, versioned and run in the existing all-or-nothing initializer.
Existing financial values remain unchanged; old customers and costs remain unknown.
Production startup requires the current schema and never automatically migrates.

Stop the application, back up using the existing management command, run the explicit
migration against the intended local database, and run integrity checks before restart.
Use the README backup/restore procedure; retain the pre-migration backup. No working
or production database is migrated automatically by this development work.

All new services derive store context server-side, use parameterized SQL and enforce
read-only/demo restrictions. New forms inherit authentication and CSRF checks. SQL
foreign keys/ownership guards prevent cross-store associations, and financial ledgers
retain immutable references. No real card fields or payment integrations are added.

## Security findings and boundaries

- Three fictional accounts exercise routes and services for each other's customer,
  supplier, PO, product-cost and expense IDs; foreign IDs return not found and do
  not mutate rows. Reports and downloads derive the store from authenticated state.
- Public demo sessions see only their fictional store. New demo pages expose no
  write forms except logout, and direct mutations remain forbidden.
- Existing authentication, opaque server-backed sessions, throttling, default route
  protection, CSRF, secure production cookies, CSP and sensitive-page no-store
  headers remain active. No new auth bypass, uploads, card fields or debug mode.
- Contact fields are optional and escaped; summary exports omit contact details.
  CSV text values neutralize formulas. Directory exports use exactly the same SQL
  filters/collation as their HTML lists, including non-ASCII names.
- Discount/cost snapshots, receiving history and expense entries have immutability
  guards. Composite foreign keys and explicit ownership guards prevent cross-store
  references. Stock receiving uses the existing atomic inventory audit/alert path.
- Existing strong production secret/absolute database path/schema/private-file
  checks still fail closed. Deployment needs HTTPS, private persistent local SQLite
  storage and private backups; local test success is not production certification.
- No runtime dependencies or new secrets were added. The final dependency audit
  reported no known vulnerabilities; this does not guarantee an absence of flaws.

## Bounds and operational limitations

Directories show 20 records per page. Customer profiles display the latest 100 sales
and refunds, with complete totals bounded at 10,000 of each. Directory exports are
bounded at 10,000 matching customers/suppliers and 10,000 linked sales/refunds.
POs support 200 lines, quantities up to 1,000,000 and stock within that same cap.
Purchasing reports are bounded at 10,000 headers and 50,000 order/receiving lines;
supplier profiles show at most 500 linked products and the latest 100 orders.
Expense lists/exports use at most 10,000 matching ledger entries, paginated by 20.
Financial reports reuse existing maximum 366-day periods, 10,000 transactions per
current/comparison period and 50,000 sale/refund lines. Exceeding a bound fails with
an explicit error rather than silently truncating financial totals.

Search is substring-based within a store; SQLite's built-in case handling applies
to customer/supplier directories and their matching exports. Indexes cover store,
status, customer history, supplier/product relationships, order/receiving dates and
expense category/date. Reports aggregate bounded Python integers to avoid floats
or SQLite SUM overflow. No cross-store caches are introduced.

Inactive draft products can be removed, but cannot be ordered. Inactive products
cannot be received; resolve product availability before confirming receiving.
Partially received POs cannot be cancelled in this MVP. Reference costs are manual,
never automatically recalculated by receiving; business owners must maintain them
for future estimates. Expense corrections use today's reversal date and the chosen
replacement date, so historical original entries remain visible. These workflows
record no supplier payments, inventory valuation adjustments or financial statements.

Deferred: secure image uploads (existing product-initial placeholders remain),
item-level promotions, free-item sales, purchase returns/supplier credits, supplier
payments, custom expense categories, advanced costing, and Day 26 visual redesign.
No automatic database reset, migration of the working database, commit, push or
deployment has been performed.
